Make safer choices online

This site is for anyone who uses email, messaging apps or websites in their daily life. It focuses on common situations where people get caught out — suspicious messages, weak passwords, rushed decisions and unsafe downloads — and shows simple steps you can take to reduce risk.

Start with the topic that matches what you're dealing with right now.

You receive a message asking you to click a link or share details. Learn how to check the sender, the link and the request before you respond, and what to do if you think you've clicked something malicious.

Password security

One leaked password can unlock several accounts. See how to reduce password reuse, use a password manager, and choose stronger passphrases without having to remember dozens of complex strings.

MFA adds a second check when you sign in. Understand what normal prompts look like, why they matter, and what to do if you get an unexpected approval request or code.

About this site

This portal offers plain‑language guidance for common cyber security issues. It is informed by widely recognised training topics: defending against phishing, using strong passwords and multi‑factor authentication, securing devices, browsing safely, and reporting incidents early. It does not replace official advice from your employer or national cyber security bodies.
